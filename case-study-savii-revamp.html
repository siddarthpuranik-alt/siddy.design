OVERVIEW

What is SAVii?

SAVii is one of the Philippines’ leading financial wellness platforms, offering salary-linked loans and other financial products to employees.

The app serves thousands of users who depend on it to access credit, manage repayments, and explore financial products — but its UI hadn’t kept up with the product’s ambitions.

View Website

THE PROBLEM

SAVii’s UI looked very outdated and lacked polish

We identified four core issues across the product cards and home screen — ranging in severity from high-priority UX failures to medium-priority visual issues.

VISUAL EXPLORATIONS

3 Directions. One brand voice.

We explored three distinct visual directions, each pushing a different interpretation of SAVii’s identity — from trustworthy and functional to bold and tech-forward.

direction 01

Iconiq

Trustworthy Clear Functional

The bet

Lead with USPs, not product names. A borrower cares about “interest from 0.5%” before they care about “Salary Loan.”

What worked

Spacious, clean layout. Flat illustrations that signal safety, important for a lending product. Cards felt calm and low-pressure.

The tension

SAVii worried existing users would lose recognition without prominent product names. They also insisted on retaining “Learn More” as an explicit CTA — we’d proposed removing it to reduce decision load.

direction 02

Edgey

Tech-forward Bold Confident

The bet

Rethink the entire card — no CTAs, just product name, key numbers, and a tappable surface. Zero choice paralysis.

What worked

Boldest visual direction. Simplified card structure was our strongest conviction — fewer choices, faster decisions.

The tension

SAVii was open to rethinking card content but not the broader visual system. “Too wild” for the existing app language. This drew a useful line: flexibility in what’s on the card, not in how the app looks overall.

direction 03

Vivid

Modern Youthful Aspirational

The bet

Give product name and USP equal weight — solve the recognition concern from Iconiq without losing the value-first approach.

What worked

3D icons and gradient headers felt premium and aligned with SAVii’s approachable brand. The balanced hierarchy landed well with the team.

The tension

We flagged dual CTAs (“Learn More” + “Apply Now”) as redundant. SAVii’s counter — repeat borrowers want a direct “Apply” shortcut without revisiting the product page. Valid, but two equally weighted buttons creates hesitation.

CHOSEN DIRECTION

Vivid hits the sweet spot between brand and ambition.

IMPACT

A visual refresh that moved the needle by 60%.

Following the redesign, SAVii saw a 60% increase in Refer and Win participation — the strongest signal that the visual refresh resonated with users.

View Website

THE PROBLEM

SAVii’s UI looked very outdated and lacked polish

We identified four core issues across the product cards and home screen — ranging in severity from high-priority UX failures to medium-priority visual issues.

High

The best selling points are the least visible

Key benefits like loan limits and zero-cost insurance are rendered in small, muted text. They get lost in the card before the user even registers them.

High

Images don’t tell the product story

Generic stock photography creates no association with the product. Users lose a visual anchor that could otherwise reinforce what they’re about to apply for.

Medium

Primary & secondary actions take up too much space

The CTAs are massive, and take away the attention from what product is selling, creating poor hierarchy.

High

Cards are dense with no clear focal point

Four content layers with no visual separation make it hard for users to know what to read first, or what the card is asking them to do.
